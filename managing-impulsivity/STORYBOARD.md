# Storyboard: The Magic of STOP, THINK & ACT!

An animated, interactive lesson for NGS Preschool Preparatory (ages 4–6), built from *Habits of Mind: Managing Impulsivity*. This file is generated from the script inside `index.html`, which is the playable version.

## Characters

- **Thinky**: A floating, glowing thinking-buddy with a traffic light on top: red = STOP, yellow = THINK, green = ACT. Guide and narrator's helper.
- **Omar**: Energetic and eager. Learns to stop before grabbing, and to filter his words.
- **Zara**: Cheerful and patient. Shows waiting in the queue and safe road crossing.
- **Mia**: Creative builder. Feels big anger when her tower falls, and learns deep breathing.
- **Leo**: Fast and funny. Learns about rules, the supermarket queue and paying first.
- **Mama (Zara's mother)**: Kind grown-up who holds hands at the road and welcomes visitors.
- **Mr. Bilal**: The friendly supermarket cashier.
