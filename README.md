## Moxel

Most analytics tools give you a dashboard full of charts and leave the thinking to you.
Moxel watches forms and funnels, finds the step where people drop off, and says what to fix.
No cookies, so no consent banner. Works with React and Next.js.

[Try it](https://www.getmoxel.com) · [npm package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/getmoxel)
