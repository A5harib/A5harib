Outer Space Adventure

An interactive space video for preschool explorers

Scene 1
CCPress play to begin our trip to outer space!

What this video covers

  • Unit 1: Universe, the Sun (Sol), the 8 planets, orbits, galaxies
  • Unit 2: Earth, day and night, the Moon and its phases, stars, constellations, the Milky Way
  • Unit 3: Astronomy, Galileo, Newton, Hubble, astronauts, space suits, living in space
  • Unit 4: Satellites, rockets, the space shuttle, moons, each planet, dwarf planets, meteorites, comets

Games from the worksheets

  • Planet Riddle: Who Am I? (Worksheet 1)
  • My Space Words (Worksheet 2)
  • The Solar System order (Worksheet 3)
  • If I Were an Alien (Worksheet 4)
  • Astronaut Application (Worksheet 5)
  • Day and Night (Worksheet 6)

Tips for the classroom

  • Play it on a big screen at circle time, one unit per week.
  • Pause on game scenes and let children take turns tapping.
  • Songs for Solar System Day: The Solar System Song and StoryBots Outer Space.
  • Keyboard: Space to play or pause, arrow keys to move between scenes.
