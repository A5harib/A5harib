Outer Space Adventure

An interactive space programme for children aged 4 to 7

Scene 1
TwinklePress play to begin our trip to outer space!

How to use it

  • Play it on a big screen at circle time. Each unit takes about one session.
  • When a question appears, the timer gives children time to think and call out. Tap an answer, or wait and the answer is shown.
  • Games wait for a child to play. Press "skip" on the pink button to move on.
  • Keyboard: Space to play or pause, arrow keys to change scene.
